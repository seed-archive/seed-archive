## 14 November 2026: version 1.0

- Added [farming](/no-mans-sky/guides/crafting/farming): every farm plant, what a harvest sells for at a space station, how long it grows, and how many plants are worth planting, with the price a crop loses as you sell it and the markup NipNip Buds take as contraband.
- Added [Interceptor prices](/no-mans-sky/guides/ships/interceptor-prices): what a claimed Sentinel Interceptor is worth by its cargo slots and its class.
- The ship builders are open: [Sentinel Interceptors](/no-mans-sky/tools/interceptor-finder), [capital freighters](/no-mans-sky/tools/capital-freighter-finder), [regular freighters](/no-mans-sky/tools/freighter-finder), [living ships](/no-mans-sky/tools/living-ship-finder), and [living frigates](/no-mans-sky/tools/living-frigate-finder), with the guides to [living ships](/no-mans-sky/guides/ships/living-ships) and [organic frigates](/no-mans-sky/guides/freighters/organic-frigates).
- A system's page now lists what floats in its space: space hulks, asteroid belts, ice fields, and whether it holds a Relic Gate.
- Every guide is counted again over more than three times as many systems, so its numbers hold closer.
- Smaller changes: every section of a guide can be linked, and farming and [exosuit modules](/no-mans-sky/guides/exosuit/exosuit-modules) link the Haz-Mat Gauntlet each way.
